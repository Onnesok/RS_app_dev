# 🚀 Mobile App Development Journey
## 6-Session Crash Course

Welcome aboard! This course isn't just about code; it's about turning your ideas into real, working apps. Over the next 6 sessions, we'll go from "How does this work?" to "Look what I built!"

Here represents our roadmap.

### 💻 Gear Check (Requirements)
To ensure smooth sailing, here's what your machine needs. While you *can* code on less, these specs will keep your frustration levels low.

| Component | Minimum (It Works) | Recommended (It Flies) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OS** | Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux | Windows 11 or latest macOS |
| **Processor** | Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 (Dual Core) | Intel i7 / Ryzen 7 / Apple M-Series |
| **RAM** | 8 GB | 16 GB (Highly Recommended) |
| **Storage** | 10 GB Free Space | SSD Storage (HDDs will be painful) |

> **💡 Pro Tip:** An **SSD** is the single biggest upgrade you can make for development. It makes launching simulators and compiling code significantly faster!

### **Class 01: Introduction & Basic Structure of an App**
**"How do apps actually work?"**
Before we write a single line of code, we need to understand the ecosystem. We'll demystify what's going on behind the screens of the apps you use every day.
*   **We'll explore:**
    *   Native vs. Cross-platform: Why we chose Flutter?
    *   The frontend (what you see) vs. the backend (the brain).
    *   APIs: How apps talk to the internet.
    *   The life of an app: From idea to app store.

### **Class 02: Dart Syntax**
**"Learning to command the computer."**
Flutter is the car, but Dart is the engine. Today, we learn the grammar of logic. Don't worry if you’ve never coded; we'll start from hello!
*   **We'll play with:**
    *   Variables: Storing information.
    *   Control Flow: Making decisions (if this, then that).
    *   Loops: Doing things repeatedly without getting tired.
    *   OOP: Thinking in "Objects" to organize our code.

### **Class 03: Basic App Architecture**
**"How to build a house that doesn't fall down."**
Writing code is easy; organizing it is the art. We'll look at how to structure a Flutter app so it stays clean and scalable, plus a sneak peek at the backend powerhouses.
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **The Widget Tree:** Understanding that *everything* is a Lego block.
    *   **Stateless vs. Stateful:** When does an app need to "change"?
    *   **Organization:** Where do I put my files?
    *   **Navigation:** Moving between screens smoothly.
    *   **The Cloud & Beyond:** Introduction to APIs, Firebase, and Google Cloud basics.

### **Class 04: Flutter Installation and Figma**
**"Getting our hands dirty."**
Half the battle is setup! We'll get your laptops ready for development. Then, we switch gears to the visual side with Figma.
*   **The Workshop:**
    *   **Flutter Install Party:** Setting up the SDK, VS Code or Android Studio.
    *   **Figma 101:** A tour of the interface. Layers, frames, and making basic shapes look good.

### **Class 05: Figma Prototyping**
**"Fake it 'til you make it."**
Before coding the UI, we visualize it. We'll turn static designs into clickable prototypes that feel like the real deal. Then, the magic happens: we turn that design into real code.
*   **What we'll make:**
    *   Components: Design once, reuse everywhere.
    *   Interactions: Connecting screens with "Smart Animate".
    *   *The "Aha!" Moment:* Previewing your design right on your phone!
    *   **Design to Code:** Translating our Figma frames into actual Flutter widgets.

### **Class 06: Project Presentation**
**"Presentation Day."**
This is it! You'll showcase your project idea. Whether it's a polished prototype or a working alpha, this is your moment to shine and get feedback.
*   **Agenda:**
    *   Single project presentation.
    *   Constructive feedback on UX/UI.
    *   Code reviews (for those who started coding).
    *   Where to go from here?

---
**👨‍🏫 Note:** Classes 02, 04, and 05 are hands-on workshops. Please bring your laptops charged and ready to create!
